Scrap Saw: Keep the Gold - Privacy Policy

Odd Beacon Games · Effective 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Scrap Saw: Keep the Gold ("the game") handles, made by Odd Beacon Games ("we", "us"). In short: we don't run any servers, we don't ask you for an account, and we don't collect your name, email or contacts. The game uses a few Google services, described below, which handle some data under Google's own policies.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (stars, bolts, unlocked survivors and tapes, purchases and settings) is saved on your device. We never receive it.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you're signed in to Google Play Games, the game saves a copy of your progress to your Google account, so it can come back on a new phone. This uses your Play Games profile and is stored by Google, not by us. You can play without signing in; your progress then stays on the device. You can delete Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or in your Google account settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional "watch an ad" rewards, and occasional full-screen ads between levels (never during play, and never after you buy Remove Ads). To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time with the privacy button on the title screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases (premium packs, Remove Ads) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game what you've bought so it can unlock it.

Crash and diagnostic data

If the game crashes, Google Play may collect a crash report (for example, device model and Android version) and show it to us in the Google Play Console, without identifying you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent: the privacy button on the title screen (where it applies).
  • Personalised ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Cloud save: don't sign in to Play Games, or delete the game's data from Play Games.
  • Everything else: uninstalling the game removes its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
